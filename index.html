Privacy Policy

Smart Invoice for Android
Last updated: 8 November 2026

1. Who we are

Smart Invoice is published by Jayavel Tech Lab (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the Smart Invoice app handles information. The package name is com.jayaveltechlab.smartinvoice.

Contact: jayaveltechlab@gmail.com

2. We do not run an account server

Smart Invoice does not ask you to create an account with us. We do not operate a server that stores your bills, customers, or photos. The information you enter is saved on your phone.

3. Information stored on your phone

When you use the app, it stores what you type or add, including:

  • Organization details, such as the shop name, address, phone, email, GSTIN, FSSAI number, and logo
  • People you bill, including name, phone, email, address, date of birth, GSTIN, and identity details you choose to enter
  • Photos and files you attach to a person, and signature drawings
  • Bills, invoice numbers, payments, item descriptions, prices, stock, and tax settings
  • Your theme, language, and other settings

This information stays in the app’s private storage on the device. Uninstalling the app removes it from the phone.

4. Camera and microphone

The camera is used only when you choose to take a logo or a person’s photo. The picture is saved in the app on your phone.

The microphone is used only when you choose voice input. Speech is turned into text by the speech-recognition service on your phone (usually Google). We do not receive or keep the audio recording. The resulting text is stored in the app if you add it to a bill or a field.

5. Optional AI mode

AI mode is off until you enter your own Google Gemini API key. The key is saved only on your phone and is not included in a backup file.

When AI mode is on and the phone is online, the words you are billing are sent to Google’s Gemini API so a reply can be returned. That processing is covered by Google’s terms and privacy policy. We do not use your bills to train our own models. You can turn AI mode off or delete the key in the app.

6. Sharing that you start

Print, WhatsApp, and other share actions send a bill only when you tap the button. The file or message then goes to the app you pick, such as WhatsApp. A backup file contains your bills, customers, photos, organization details, and settings, but not the AI key. You choose where that file is saved or sent. We do not upload it for you.

7. Google Play

The app may ask Google Play whether a newer version is available. Barcode scanning uses Google Play services on the device to read a code. Google’s privacy policy applies to those services. We do not add advertising or our own analytics SDK.

8. Android backup

If you have Android backup turned on, Google may include this app’s local data in the backup of your phone. That backup is controlled by your Google account settings, not by us.

9. Children

Smart Invoice is a business billing tool. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
